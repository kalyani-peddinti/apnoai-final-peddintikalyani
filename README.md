**Insight 1 (Revenue):** Q2 sales of ₹16.3M are spread evenly across categories (Furniture ~₹3.5M highest, Electronics ~₹3.0M lowest), but Sunday is ~33% weaker than Wednesday, so a weekend promotion could capture unused demand.

**Insight 2 (Profitability)**: Q2 profit was ₹4.4M at a 27.18% margin, but North (~₹0.79M) trails Central (~₹0.93M) by about 15%, so discounts and costs in North and South need review.

**Insight 3 (Customers):** About 1.2K orders averaged ~₹13.6K in sales each, so a small drop in repeat buying would noticeably hurt profit, and retention matters more than order volume.
